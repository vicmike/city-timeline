## Corrections applied to digitized Chandler

## Cross-check vs Wikipedia's Chandler column (2)

- 200 BC: digitized Chandler's #1 is xian (400,000); Wikipedia's Chandler column says Pataliputra (350,000)
  - **Reviewed (caveat):** The digitized Chandler dataset (Reba et al. 2016) lists Chang'an at 400,000 in 200 BC, but Wikipedia's transcription of Chandler (1987) names Pataliputra (350,000) as the largest city that year. Chang'an only became the Han capital in 202 BC, so treat its ranking this year with caution. Not resolved without the book itself.
- 1000: cordoba is #1 in both, but digitized value 450,000 ≠ Wikipedia's 350,000
  - **Reviewed (caveat):** The digitized dataset gives Córdoba 450,000 in 1000, the figure widely quoted from Chandler; Wikipedia's list of largest cities gives Chandler's figure as 350,000. Either way it is Chandler's largest city that year.

## Possible gaps in the digitized data (4)

- AD 361: Luoyang|China not recorded, but interpolating AD 100 420,000 → AD 500 200,000 gives ~276,450, which would rank in the top 6 (cut-off 150,000)
  - **Reviewed (no-action):** Interpolation is misleading here: Luoyang was sacked in 311 (Disaster of Yongjia) and lost its capital status, so it was far smaller in 361 than a straight line from AD 100 to AD 500 implies.
- AD 500: Xian|China not recorded, but interpolating AD 361 80,000 → AD 622 400,000 gives ~250,421, which would rank in the top 5 (cut-off 150,000)
  - **Reviewed (no-action):** Chang'an was a regional city in 500; its growth to 400,000 followed the Sui rebuilding of the capital (Daxing) in 582. Interpolating from 361 to 622 overstates 500.
- 1000: Xian|China not recorded, but interpolating AD 900 500,000 → 1077 45,000 gives ~242,938, which would rank in the top 5 (cut-off 175,000)
  - **Reviewed (no-action):** Chang'an was largely destroyed and abandoned as capital in 904, so it was far smaller in 1000 than interpolating from 900 suggests.
- 1100: Kyoto|Japan not recorded, but interpolating 1000 175,000 → 1150 150,000 gives ~158,333, which would rank in the top 6 (cut-off 150,000)
  - **Reviewed (caveat):** Heian-kyō (Kyoto) is not recorded for 1100 in the digitized dataset. Chandler has it at 175,000 in 1000 and 150,000 in 1150, so it probably belongs in this year's top 5 (tied around 150,000).
